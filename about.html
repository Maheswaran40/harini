About Us

Simple shopping, simple learning.

ShopEasy is a front-end e-commerce learning project.

Company Introduction

ShopEasy is a demo online store designed to show how a small e-commerce website can be built with browser technologies only.

Our Mission

Make shopping concepts easy to understand through clear product cards, search, cart and wishlist features.

Our Vision

Help beginners understand how HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, JSON and localStorage work together.

Why Choose Us?

Clean UI, responsive design and simple JavaScript logic make this project easy to explore and extend.
